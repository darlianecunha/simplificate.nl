About

Founder-turned-operator, still obsessed with the funnel.

Through Simplificate, I help teams simplify growth. Growth marketing consultant with 6+ years across paid media, brand, content, and lifecycle for B2C e-commerce, agency clients, and B2B tech companies.

My background includes founding and scaling an e-commerce brand, managing programmatic and paid media campaigns for global brands such as American Express, Amazon Prime Video, Ernst & Young, and Dr. Martens. At OVSoftware, I took end-to-end ownership of growth, brand, demand and lead generation strategies, from planning and messaging to campaign execution, testing, optimization, and reporting. As Growth Marketing Manager at Faslet, I led paid, organic, lifecycle, and go-to-market initiatives through continuous experimentation.

I build custom growth roadmaps tailored to each business's specific needs using a structured growth methodology. Experienced in reading campaign performance through the lens of behavioral psychology and data to understand how people think, decide, and act. Quick to adapt to new tools and processes, I use AI tools and workflows daily to produce content, run experiments, and scale execution.
